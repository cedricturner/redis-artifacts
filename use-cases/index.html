03 PHYSICAL AI

Home robot butler

drawn three ways

Clears the table, loads the dishwasher, puts the plates away.

NEEDS: SENSOR INGEST / A MEMORY THAT ANSWERS / A DECISION INSIDE THE DEADLINE

WHAT IT TAKES

A HOME ROBOT NEEDS

  1  somewhere to put what it just saw
         arriving constantly, never stopping

  2  somewhere to keep what it knows
         the room, the people, what happened last time

  3  an answer fast enough to act on
         before the arm has finished moving

  4  the decision handed back
         and kept, so the next one is faster

FOUR REQUIREMENTS. NONE OF THEM ARE ABOUT THE ROBOT.

THE LOOP

LOOP

  1  sensors  -> write what just happened
               cameras, microphones, joints

  2  robot    -> what do I know about this?
               needs an answer in under 10 ms

  3  store    -> the state of the room right now
               what this object was last time
               what the house is supposed to contain

  4  robot    -> decide. move. put the plate away.

  5  store    -> keep what happened
               so the next plate is faster

THE ROBOT IS TWO LINES OF THIS. THE OTHER THREE ARE A DATA PROBLEM.

DATA COMING IN
Thousands of events a second from cameras, microphones and joints. It never pauses and it cannot be allowed to queue up behind a write.
A MEMORY TO ASK
What is this object. What happened last time I tried this. Who lives here. Asked as a question, answered by similarity, not by scanning a table.
AN ANSWER IN TIME
Single digit milliseconds. Past that, the plate has already moved and the answer is about a room that no longer exists.
THE DECISION BACK
Pushed to the robot the moment it exists. If the robot has to poll for it, the polling interval becomes the reaction time.
THE BORING DATA
The floor plan, the household, the product catalogue. It lives in systems you already run and has to arrive here fresh, not nightly.
